Bundled third-party driver: VB-Audio Virtual Cable
====================================================

Poise Voice Isolator routes system audio through VB-CABLE
(CABLE Input / CABLE Output), a virtual audio driver by VB-Audio
Software (V.Burel). Origin: https://vb-audio.com/Cable/

VB-CABLE is donationware: it is free to use, and all participations
to VB-Audio are welcome. If you find VB-CABLE useful, please consider
donating for a license on their webshop:
https://shop.vb-audio.com/

This installer bundles the base VB-CABLE driver package with VB-Audio's
permission for distribution (silent install, only when CABLE endpoints
are not already present on your system). CABLE A+B / C+D are NOT
included. VB-Audio, VB-CABLE and related names belong to their owner.
