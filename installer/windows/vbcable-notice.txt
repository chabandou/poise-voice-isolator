Third-party software: VB-CABLE
==============================

Poise includes VB-CABLE, a virtual audio cable developed by
VB-Audio Software.

Origin of VB-CABLE: https://www.vb-cable.com/
VB-CABLE is donationware, all participations are welcome.
Licensing and donations: https://vb-audio.com/Services/licensing.htm

VB-CABLE is a third-party component and is not covered by
Poise's MIT licence.

This installer bundles the base VB-CABLE package and runs its
setup silently, only when CABLE endpoints are not already
present on your system. VB-CABLE A+B / C+D are NOT included.
